How the Image Reveal Works

When a goal has a photo, it starts hidden behind a piggy-bank cover. As kids save money, the cover lifts and the photo gradually reveals from bottom to top. Hit 100% and the full image appears with a confetti celebration!

In the demo: Sophie is saving for a New Skateboard (36% revealed). Tap the goal on her Home screen to see the photo gradually appearing as she saves.

Things to Try

  • Hit 100% on a goal for a confetti celebration
